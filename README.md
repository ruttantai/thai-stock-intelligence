# Investor Behavior & Exit Risk Intelligence (Discontinued) 
### For Thai Equity Markets

A Thailand-focused analytics application that provides structured insights into **investor behavior, capital flows, and exit risk conditions** in Thai listed stocks.

The system leverages Thailand-specific datasets — particularly **NVDR and foreign flow data** — to generate interpretable indicators that help users understand:

- Who is accumulating or distributing
- Whether liquidity supports safe exit
- Current behavioral market regimes

This application prioritizes **clarity, interpretability, and structural risk visibility** over price prediction.

---

## Overview

Retail investors often lack visibility into:

- Flow dynamics between foreign, institutional, and retail participants
- Liquidity fragility during volatility
- Structural risks such as trading halts or forced exits

This application aggregates and transforms flow and liquidity data into structured signals designed to improve situational awareness in Thai equities.

---

## Core Modules

### 1. Investor Accumulation Signals (IBI Core)

Analyzes participation across:

- Foreign investors
- Institutions
- Retail investors

#### Features

- Accumulation and distribution scoring
- Quiet accumulation detection
- Flow divergence analysis
- Interpretable signal labels

#### Example Outputs

- Foreign Accumulation
- Institutional Distribution
- Retail Dominance
- Mixed Flow Divergence

All outputs are descriptive and rules-based to maintain transparency.

---

### 2. Liquidity & Exit Risk Analytics

Evaluates the tradability and structural vulnerability of a stock.

#### Metrics Include

- Tradability Score
- Liquidity Fragility Index
- Volume Shock Sensitivity
- Trading Halt Risk Indicators
- Margin / Pledge Exposure (if data available)

The goal is to assess whether exit conditions may deteriorate under stress.

---

### 3. Market Regime Classification

Provides descriptive regime labels at:

- Market level
- Sector level
- Stock level

#### Regime Examples

- Accumulation Phase
- Distribution Phase
- Speculative Activity
- Liquidity Contraction
- Foreign Risk-Off Environment

These labels summarize behavioral and liquidity conditions without making forward price predictions.

## Intended Users

- Retail investors seeking structural flow insight
- Quantitative traders requiring flow-based features
- Risk-conscious portfolio managers
- Thai market researchers

---

## Non-Goals

This application does not:

- Provide fundamental valuation analysis
- Generate price targets
- Offer automated trading execution
- Use black-box predictive models

---

## Summary

Investor Behavior & Exit Risk Intelligence is a focused analytics system for Thai equities that transforms flow and liquidity data into structured, interpretable signals to improve market awareness and risk visibility.
